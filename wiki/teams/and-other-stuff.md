# And Other Stuff (AOS)

**Canonical id:** `team_and-other-stuff`
**Type:** organization · **Website:** [andotherstuff.org](https://andotherstuff.org/)

## Summary
And Other Stuff (AOS) is a collective of people who build, imagine, organize, and experiment. Together, they create and steward **freedom tech** — open tools, protocols, and applications that strengthen human liberty, enhance the Commons, and empower people to build their own futures.

## Hubs
Work flows through three interconnected hubs:

- [[teams/lab]] — *The Lab*: exploration, research, art of the possible.
- [[teams/studio]] — *The Studio*: ideas moving toward real-world impact.
- [[teams/foundry]] — *The Foundry*: projects the collective supports and stewards.

## Foundry projects
See [[projects/index]] — the Foundry currently supports 13 projects spanning Nostr, Bitcoin, Cashu, messaging, video, activism, and developer tools.
