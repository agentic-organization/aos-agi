# Nutstash

**Status:** active · **Ecosystem:** [[projects/cashu]] · **Owner:** external (see below)
**Language:** TypeScript · **Kind:** wallet (web)

## Summary
Feature-rich Cashu web wallet with multimint support and the ability to send/receive tokens over Nostr. Built in TypeScript on top of [[projects/cashu-ts]]. Maintained by [[people/gandlafbtc]], who also maintains several sibling ecosystem tools (Proxnut, cashu-tools, cashu-brrr, etc.).
