# TollGate

**Status:** active · **Ecosystem:** [[projects/cashu]] · **Owner:** external (see below)
**Kind:** application (network access)

## Summary
Turns any **WiFi router into a decentralized ISP** using Bitcoin and Cashu ecash. Instead of ISP contracts, users pay per-byte or per-session in sats via Cashu tokens, and routers can be operated by anyone willing to share their uplink. One of the more provocative ecash applications — a practical take on internet access as a commodity paid for in real time.
