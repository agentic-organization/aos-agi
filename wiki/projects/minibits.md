# Minibits

**Status:** active · **Ecosystem:** [[projects/cashu]] · **Owner:** external (see below)
**Language:** TypeScript / React Native · **Kind:** wallet (mobile)

## Summary
Mobile Cashu wallet focused on performance and usability. One of the earliest and most heavily used mobile Cashu wallets. Maintained by [[people/minibits-cash]] outside the cashubtc org.
