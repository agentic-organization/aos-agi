# Athenut

**Status:** active · **Ecosystem:** [[projects/cashu]] · **Owner:** external (see below)
**Kind:** application (search)

## Summary
**Privacy-preserving web search powered by Kagi**, paid for with Cashu ecash. Users perform premium Kagi searches without creating an account or revealing identity — they pay per-query in sats via Cashu. Comprises a frontend and a dedicated mint.
