**Status:** historical / being revived · **Ecosystem:** [[projects/cashu]] · **Owner team:** [[teams/cashu-core]]

## Summary
Android/iOS Cashu wallet. The original [eNuts](https://github.com/cashubtc/eNuts) is listed as **currently unmaintained** on awesome-cashu; a successor effort [eNuts 2](https://github.com/cashubtc/eNuts/tree/master) is being resurrected on a branch of the same repo. Historical importance: eNuts was one of the first broadly-marketed mobile Cashu wallets.
