# Cashu Redeem

**Status:** active · **Ecosystem:** [[projects/cashu]] · **Owner team:** [[teams/cashu-core]]
**Kind:** application (redemption tool)

## Summary
A **browser-only web tool** to redeem any Cashu token directly into a Lightning wallet, without installing a Cashu wallet. Useful for recipients who are sent ecash but have no interest in holding it.

Two active deployments share the lineage:
- `redeem.cashu.me/` — the current official surface; handles v4 tokens, emoji-encoded tokens, and stores change in localStorage.
- `cashu-redeem.vercel.app/` — an older sibling.
