# Zeus

**Status:** active · **Ecosystem:** [[projects/cashu]] · **Owner:** external (see below)
**Kind:** wallet (Lightning + Cashu)

## Summary
Well-established Bitcoin **Lightning wallet** with Cashu ecash integration added. Zeus is primarily an LN node-management and LN wallet app with a large pre-existing user base; the Cashu integration means Cashu tokens can be held and spent alongside Lightning channels in a familiar UI.
