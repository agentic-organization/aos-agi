# Soapbox (Mastodon-fork client)

**Status:** legacy / maintenance · **Hub:** [[teams/foundry]] · **Owner team:** [[teams/soapbox]] · **Organization:** [[teams/and-other-stuff]]

## Summary
The **original** Soapbox client — a fork of the Mastodon (Pleroma-compatible) frontend, "software for the next generation of social media." This is the project the team and org take their name from; active-development center of gravity has since shifted to [[projects/ditto]] (Nostr-native), but the legacy client still sits in the org and historical contributions are extensive.
