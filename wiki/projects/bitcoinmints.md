# Bitcoinmints

**Status:** active · **Ecosystem:** [[projects/cashu]] · **Owner:** external (see below)

## Summary
**Mint discovery and reputation index** — a public list of ecash mints with their supported NUTs, uptime, and community reviews. The canonical place to find a Cashu mint to use and to evaluate its operational posture. Maintained by MakePrisms (same org behind [[projects/agicash]]).
