# npub.cash

**Status:** active · **Ecosystem:** [[projects/cashu]] · **Owner team:** [[teams/cashu-core]]
**Kind:** application (LN address provider)

## Summary
**Lightning-address provider for Nostr pubkeys**, backed by a Cashu mint. A user's Nostr pubkey becomes a Lightning address (`<npub>@npub.cash`); received Lightning payments are deposited as Cashu tokens that the user can claim into any Cashu wallet. Hosted in `cashubtc/`.
