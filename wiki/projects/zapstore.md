# Zapstore

**Status:** active · **Hub:** [[teams/foundry]] · **Organization:** [[teams/and-other-stuff]]

## Summary
The open app store powered by your social network. Discover apps through people you trust and see what your network recommends. Releases are cryptographically signed by developers, so you always know exactly who built the software. Pay developers directly in bitcoin with zero fees.
