# Organization Wiki — Index

The living Markdown knowledge base of the organization. Pages are short, densely interlinked, and continuously evolved by the agent.

## Sections

- [[people/]] — One page per person. Roles, teams, skills, collaborators, systems owned.
- [[teams/]] — Teams (explicit and implicit). Members, repos, channels, products.
- [[projects/]] — Projects. Goals, systems, owners, contributors, risks, required capabilities.
- [[repositories/]] — Repositories. Purpose, owners, contributors, related systems/projects.
- [[products/]] — Products. Component projects/repos, owners, lifecycle.
- [[decisions/]] — Decision records.
- [[communication/]] — Channels, threads, and recurring conversations.
- [[systems/]] — Running services and systems. Owners, dependencies.
- [[architecture/]] — Architecture overviews spanning multiple systems.
- [[timelines/]] — Temporal views of org evolution.
- [[concepts/]] — Cross-cutting concepts and recurring patterns.
- [[glossary/]] — Short definitional entries.
- [[skills/]] — Skills. Experts, tools, teams, repos, org importance.
- [[tools/]] — Tools (operational). Skill mappings, users, related systems.

## Linking conventions

Pages use wikilinks:

- `[[people/alice-smith]]`
- `[[teams/infrastructure]]`
- `[[projects/payment-platform]]`
- `[[repositories/infra-deploy]]`
- `[[skills/kubernetes]]`
- `[[tools/kubectl]]`

All pages should:

- Stay compact (focused, composable).
- Link aggressively to related entities.
- Preserve historical observations alongside current state.
- Cite evidence where possible (raw source reference or graph edge).
